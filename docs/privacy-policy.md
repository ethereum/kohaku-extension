## Summary

UBAMM Wallet is a non-custodial browser wallet. Your seed phrases, private keys, password, and signing credentials are stored locally in your browser extension storage and are not intentionally sent to us or to third parties.

UBAMM Wallet connects to blockchains, RPC providers, relayers, bundlers, paymasters, token metadata services, privacy protocol services, hardware wallet providers, and optional crash reporting services to provide wallet functionality.

We do not sell user data. We do not use user data for advertising. We do not use user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Information Stored Locally

UBAMM Wallet may store the following information in your browser extension storage:

- encrypted seed phrases, encrypted private keys, and encrypted wallet metadata;
- account addresses, account labels, preferences, connected dapps, and selected networks;
- transaction history, pending transaction state, signed message history, and activity state;
- locally generated privacy protocol notes, keys, nullifier-related data, and operation state;
- extension settings such as theme, lock state, log level, and crash analytics preference.

This data remains on your device unless it is needed to perform a feature you request, such as checking balances, connecting to a dapp, simulating a transaction, broadcasting a transaction, using a relayer, or using a privacy protocol.

## Information Sent to Third Parties

To provide wallet functionality, UBAMM Wallet may send wallet addresses, chain IDs, token addresses, transaction data, signatures, dapp origins, RPC requests, and related technical metadata to service providers. Which services receive data depends on the features you use.

These services may include:

- blockchain RPC providers and light-client/proving infrastructure;
- block explorers and token metadata services;
- bundlers, paymasters, relayers, and transaction simulation services;
- swap and bridge providers;
- Privacy Pools, Railgun, ASP, relayer, and indexing services;
- hardware wallet connection services such as Ledger, Trezor, and GridPlus/Lattice integrations;
- crash reporting infrastructure, only if crash analytics is enabled.

Blockchain transactions and signatures you broadcast are public on the relevant blockchain. Public blockchains are not controlled by UBAMM Wallet and may permanently expose addresses, amounts, token identifiers, contract interactions, timing, and other transaction metadata.

## Crash Analytics

Crash analytics are off by default. If you enable crash analytics, UBAMM Wallet may send error reports, stack traces, extension version, browser/runtime metadata, environment tags, and a pseudonymous extension instance identifier to our crash reporting provider.

We do not intentionally include seed phrases, private keys, passwords, or wallet addresses in crash reports. You can turn crash analytics off in the extension settings.

## Dapp Connections

When you connect UBAMM Wallet to a website or decentralized application, the extension may store the dapp origin, the accounts you approved for that dapp, permissions granted to that dapp, and request history needed to process wallet interactions.

Connected dapps may learn your public wallet address and any transaction or signature data you approve. Review each dapp before connecting or signing.

## Security

UBAMM Wallet is non-custodial. You are responsible for safeguarding your seed phrase, private keys, password, devices, and browser profile.

We use reasonable technical safeguards designed to protect extension data, including local encryption for sensitive key material. No wallet can guarantee complete security, and third-party services may have their own security practices and privacy policies.

## Data Retention

Data stored locally remains in your browser profile until you remove it, reset the wallet, uninstall the extension, clear browser extension storage, or delete your browser profile.

Data transmitted to third-party services is retained according to those providers' policies. Public blockchain data may be permanent.

## Your Choices

You can:

- avoid connecting to dapps you do not trust;
- remove dapp permissions inside the wallet where supported;
- change RPC providers and network settings where supported;
- disable crash analytics;
- uninstall the extension or clear browser extension storage to remove local wallet data from the browser.

Before clearing storage or uninstalling the extension, make sure you have backed up your seed phrase or recovery material. Without it, you may permanently lose access to your wallet.

## Children

UBAMM Wallet is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
